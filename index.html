Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about locking your reward allocation today.

How does Reward Connection work? ▼

Brands and sponsors provide promotional budgets to gather consumer feedback and engagement. Reward Connection secures these allocations and distributes authentic gift cards, vouchers, and direct cash rewards to qualified participants upon completion of quick tasks.

Is it completely free to claim these gift cards and cash? ▼

Yes, claiming is 100% free. You will never be asked to pay any money or subscription fee to receive your reward allocation.

How quickly do I receive my digital code or payment? ▼

Digital gift cards and PayPal transfers are dispatched immediately following partner verification. Most participants receive their digital voucher PIN code via email within 5 to 15 minutes.

Why are there limited spots remaining per card? ▼

Each retail partner allocates a fixed daily quota of promotional gift cards to prevent automated abuse and ensure genuine consumer distribution. Once the daily spots reach zero, that offer closes until the next 24-hour cycle.
